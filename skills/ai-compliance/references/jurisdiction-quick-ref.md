## Key Compliance Deadlines (sorted by urgency)

Check `tools/data/deadline-data.json` for the full list with exact dates.
The Global Overview file has a comprehensive timeline section.

Priority deadlines to flag (as of October 2026):
- EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency and penalties: in force since August 2, 2026
- EU machine-readable marking grace period ends: December 2, 2026
- Australia Privacy Act ADM transparency: December 10, 2026
- US Jan 1, 2027: Colorado SB 26-189, NY RAISE Act, Illinois SB 315, CA CCPA ADMT rules
- India DPDP core obligations: May 13, 2027
- EU AI Act high-risk (Annex III): December 2, 2027 (delayed by Digital Omnibus)
- EU AI Act high-risk (Annex I products): August 2, 2028
- South Korea AI Basic Act: in force since January 22, 2026; fines deferred to about January 2027
